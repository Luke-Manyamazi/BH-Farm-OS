# BUILD PROMPT — BOROMA HILLS FARM MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Build a complete, production-ready web application called **Boroma Hills Farm Management System**, also branded as **BH Farm OS**.

This application is for managing a real mixed smallholder farm called **Boroma Hills Farm**, located in Zimbabwe.

The application must be designed as a serious farm management platform rather than a simple CRUD dashboard.

The system must help the farm owner and workers manage:

* Free-range chickens
* Broilers
* Goats
* Pigs
* Fish
* Field crops
* Household vegetable garden
* Commercial vegetable production
* Future greenhouse
* Orchard
* Water and irrigation
* Farm infrastructure
* Feed and agricultural inventory
* Purchases
* Sales
* Expenses
* Farm tasks
* Animal health
* Crop health
* Production records
* Farm profitability
* Reports
* Future IoT/sensor integration
* Future AI-assisted farm management

The application must be mobile-first because farm workers will primarily use smartphones while working around the farm.

---

# 1. PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

BH Farm OS should answer these questions at any time:

1. What animals do we currently have?
2. How many chickens, broilers, pigs, goats and fish do we have?
3. Which animals are pregnant?
4. Which animals are sick?
5. Which vaccinations and treatments are due?
6. How much feed do we have?
7. How much feed are we consuming?
8. How much have we spent?
9. How much have we sold?
10. Which farm enterprise is profitable?
11. Which crops are currently planted?
12. When were they planted?
13. What inputs were used?
14. When should crops be irrigated?
15. What was harvested?
16. What was the yield?
17. How much water is available?
18. What farm tasks are due today?
19. What problems need attention?
20. What is the overall financial position of the farm?

The system must make farm management data-driven.

* Quantities cannot be negative.
* Expenses cannot have negative amounts.
* Sale quantity cannot exceed available inventory where inventory tracking applies.
